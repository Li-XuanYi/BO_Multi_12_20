\title{LLMBO-MO: Large Language Model-Augmented Multiobjective Bayesian
Optimization for Simulation-Based Battery Fast-Charging Protocol Design}

\author{Anonymous Authors}

\markboth{IEEE TRANSACTIONS ON TRANSPORTATION ELECTRIFICATION}%
{Anonymous \MakeLowercase{\textit{et al.}}: LLMBO-MO}

\maketitle

\begin{abstract}
Designing multistage battery fast-charging protocols is an expensive
multiobjective problem because charging time, temperature rise, and
degradation-related behavior must be considered under encoded operating
limits. We present LLMBO-MO, a decomposition-based Bayesian optimization
workflow in which language-model output is a screened search signal, never an
objective observation. The model supplies a diversity-aware warm start and a
weight-conditioned preference that either augments the expected-improvement
search or induces a budgeted posterior-covariance mean shift without changing
covariance. Five paired seeds with 56 simulator calls give final
benchmark-scaled hypervolumes of $0.3835\pm0.0079$ for LLMBO-MO and
$0.3853\pm0.0094$ for ParEGO on Chen2020, and $1.8684\pm0.0027$ versus
$1.5866\pm0.0130$ on Ecker2015. No mean lift is accepted in these main runs;
they evaluate screened initialization and early preference-conditioned search
under plain expected improvement. Results are descriptive, simulation-only,
and use an uncalibrated degradation proxy in arbitrary units.
\end{abstract}

\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

LLMBO-MO screens language-model proposals while reserving objective updates for
the simulator. Across five paired seeds and 56 evaluations, its observed mean
sHV is similar to ParEGO on Chen2020 and higher in all five Ecker2015 pairs.
The main study uses warm start and candidate-pool expansion under plain EI;
the same-batch study separately tests the posterior-covariance lift. These
results remain descriptive.

The study is simulation-only, uses an uncalibrated degradation proxy and
different LLM backends, and provides no physical validation. Future work
requires matched configurations, a modern hypervolume-based baseline, and
hardware replay with a calibrated degradation endpoint.

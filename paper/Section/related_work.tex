\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

\subsection{Battery Fast-Charging Optimization}

Fast-charging design has been studied with electrochemical--thermal--aging
models, closed-loop cell experiments, and data-driven search
~\cite{ref2,ref3,ref15}. BO is attractive when a protocol evaluation is costly:
Jiang \textit{et al.} compared acquisition rules for fast-charging design
~\cite{ref7}, and Wang and Jiang combined Chebyshev decomposition with
constrained BO for time--degradation trade-offs~\cite{ref8}. More recent work
has accelerated the numerical search~\cite{ref19}, used early-life
predictors~\cite{ref18}, and incorporated a semi-empirical aging prior
~\cite{ref31}. In contrast, our health-related objective is an uncalibrated
simulator ranking proxy.

\subsection{Multiobjective Bayesian Optimization}

ParEGO converts a multiobjective problem into a sequence of augmented
Tchebycheff subproblems~\cite{ref5}, whereas constrained BO can model expensive
inequality constraints probabilistically~\cite{ref26}. Direct
hypervolume-based alternatives include differentiable qEHVI~\cite{ref27} and
qNEHVI for uncertain or noisy Pareto fronts~\cite{ref28}. Evolutionary methods
such as NSGA-II provide a nonprobabilistic comparison~\cite{ref6}. The present
archive uses sequential ParEGO; qEHVI and qNEHVI were not run and are not
retroactively reported.

\subsection{LLM-Assisted Bayesian Optimization}

LLAMBO uses LLMs for warm starting, surrogate-related prediction, and candidate
sampling~\cite{ref9}; BORA represents hypotheses about promising regions in a
language-assisted loop~\cite{ref10}. LGBO converts an LLM region preference
into a surrogate-mean shift~\cite{ref17}, while LABO treats inexpensive LLM
predictions as a distinct fidelity in a joint surrogate~\cite{ref30}. These
mechanisms assign different evidential roles to language-model output. LLMBO-MO
keeps the evaluated objective database simulator-only: an LLM proposal may
affect initialization or acquisition, but it never becomes an objective
observation or reduces the reported GP uncertainty.

\subsection{Research Gap and Positioning}

Transfer BO can reuse observations from related tasks through hierarchical or
boosted GP models~\cite{ref29}. Our archived Ecker2015 comparison does not
perform that form of statistical transfer: no Chen2020 observation is inserted
into the Ecker2015 GP. It instead tests whether the same optimization workflow
can be executed under a second simulator parameterization, while also changing
the recorded LLM variant. Accordingly, this paper positions LLMBO-MO as an
evidence-separated workflow study rather than a claim of cell-independent
transfer. Its emphasis is traceability from each language-based search signal
to the simulator evaluation it influenced.
